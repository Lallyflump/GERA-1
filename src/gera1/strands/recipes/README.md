# Recipes & Product Library

Implemented: bounded local JSON collection, exact product-to-recipe version lookup,
explicit whole-batch requirement arithmetic and evidence/unknowns. Capability:
`recipes:product_requirements:read`. Source records carry provenance references.
No RAG, recipe writing/approval workflow, supplier costing or food-safety certification.

Product specifications and recipe quantities stay here; physical stock remains with
Bobathan, money with Saul and coordination with Toby. Missing recipe/yield/quantity
or unit compatibility cannot become invented requirements. Synthetic examples are
drafts and are not Lisa’s recipes. See docs/LOCAL_BROWNIE_FOUNDATIONS.md.
